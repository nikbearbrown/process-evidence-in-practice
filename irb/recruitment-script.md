# Recruitment script (draft for the IRB)

> **Status (2026-11-22):** written for the earlier study design, which validated a learning score against a transfer check and used Fall 2026 records. Paper 2 is now a classroom study of the Frictional audit. What changes in this file is listed in [irb-protocol-notes.md](../irb-protocol-notes.md) §11.

## Executive summary

**What this is:** what the third party says in class and sends by email to invite students into the research, for next semester's courses and for past courses.

**Why read it:** the IRB reviews recruitment text as closely as the consent form. The script has to make clear that the instructor is not the one asking, that nothing about the course changes, and that the instructor cannot see who says yes.

**What it decides:** recruitment happens at the **end** of the semester, following Northeastern's guidance on recruiting your own students. For Spring 2027 the third party visits each class in the last week (April 20 to 23, 2027) with the instructor out of the room, collects the forms, and keeps them sealed until final grades are submitted (May 4, 2027). After that, students who were absent get the same invitation by email. For Fall 2026, the invitation goes by email only, after grades are submitted (December 22, 2026) and after the DHR's determination. If the DHR prefers email-only recruitment after grades for Spring as well, use the email version for both.

## Spring 2027: in class, last week of classes (third party; instructor not present), about 5 minutes

> Hi, I'm [name]. I'm [role] and I'm not involved in teaching or grading this course. Professor Brown has left the room so that he doesn't see who takes part.
>
> Professor Brown is doing research on how learning shows up in the *process* of coursework, now that AI can produce finished work. All the activities you did this term (the confidence ratings on quizzes, the review quizzes, the hints, your Frictional log, the in-class check) were part of the course for everyone. The research question is only whether your records from those activities may be used in a study, with your name removed.
>
> Saying yes or no makes no difference to your grade or anything else. I will seal the forms today, and nobody opens them until after final grades have been submitted. Professor Brown [will not see the list at all / will see it only after grades are in].
>
> The form has separate boxes for using your records, for quoting your Frictional log, and for describing your project as a case study. You can say yes to the first and no to the others, or no to everything. Handing in a blank form is fine too.
>
> Questions about the study go to [researcher email]. Questions about your rights go to Northeastern's Department of Human Research, IRBReview@northeastern.edu.

>
> This is entirely optional and has no effect on anything. Professor Brown will not know who replied. The form, with the details and separate boxes for quoting and case studies, is here: [link]. Please reply by [date].
