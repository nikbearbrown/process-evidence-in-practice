## STRUCTURAL DIAGNOSIS

## STATISTICAL INTEGRITY

## RESULTS & DISCUSSION
No participants, data, empirical figures, or effects were available. Section 5 keeps selection, reviewer familiarity, and order effects distinct.

## WRITING AS DESIGN
Replace any remaining “changed assessment” or “saved time” wording in future working instruments with descriptive review changes and research-review time.

## ETHICAL SCREENING
The de-identification standard is operational, not a synonym for anonymity. Verify recognition pathways, team permissions, service access and the independent timing of consent before any recruitment. No sensitive records were consulted for this review.

## RANKED IMPROVEMENTS
1. [CRITICAL] Section 2: obtain written institutional determination and any required approvals before recruitment, access, or processing, or defer.
2. [MAJOR] Sections 2–3: define what can be independently checked without student identity or outside tools.

## WHAT WORKS
